\title{Measure-Valued Stochastic Control and Matching in Spatial Forestry Markets}
\author{EA Forests Research}
\date{October 2026}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
We formulate a partially observed stochastic control problem for a spatial
forestry market. The physical state is a finite measure over tree attributes,
locations, log recoveries, processor specifications, and operating capacities.
Conditional McKean--Vlasov dynamics describe growth and quality under common and
idiosyncratic noise. Harvest and bucking induce a finite log measure. A
capacitated partial-transport problem allocates heterogeneous logs to processors
through spatial operators, and its dual variables measure the marginal value of
compatible supply, demand, and capacity. The decision state is the joint
posterior over physical state and structural parameters. We give the associated
belief-state recursion, a Wasserstein HJB subproblem, a risk-sensitive extension,
and a finite-dimensional commercial projection. An East African
prior-predictive calibration illustrates the projection and records the remaining
identification requirements.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} stochastic control; McKean--Vlasov dynamics;
Wasserstein geometry; unbalanced optimal transport; Bayesian experimental
design; forestry markets.
