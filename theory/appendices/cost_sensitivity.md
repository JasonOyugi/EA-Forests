# Structural cost sensitivity from the working paper

The paper holds the 100k-ha anchor fixed and varies the exponent on the variable
coverage component. Reported values are:

| Variable exponent | 500k OPEX | 1m OPEX |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.95 | USD 422k | USD 664k |
| 1.00 | USD 444k | USD 725k |
| 1.05 | USD 467k | USD 793k |
| 1.15 | USD 521k | USD 957k |
| 1.05 plus USD 100k geography block at 1m | USD 467k | USD 893k |

These are sensitivity outputs from the working paper, not observed costs.

The table makes two structural points:

1. small changes in the variable exponent matter materially at large scale;
2. geography-specific fixed blocks can offset apparent economies of scale.

A production cost model should therefore report both the fixed/variable
structure and uncertainty around scale coefficients rather than a single cost
per hectare.
