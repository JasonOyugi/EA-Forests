# Calibration interpretation checks

## 1. The model reports 72% probability of positive operating surplus at 1m ha.
Can we say there is a 72% chance the real business will be profitable at 1m ha?

No. The reported probability is conditional on the prior-predictive model and
its uncertainty distributions. Structural discrepancy and unvalidated priors
remain.

## 2. Median operating surplus improves with scale. Does that prove hectares
cause profitability?

No. Scale is mapped into adoption, visible supply, and cost through model
assumptions. The structural theory says commercially useful supply is produced
by tree/log/matching operators, not by hectares alone.

## 3. A pure-power cost fit gives beta near 0.47. Can we conclude marginal cost
is strongly sublinear?

No. The same three anchors are compatible with a large fixed cost plus an almost
linear variable component. More structure or more observations are required.

## 4. Why retain these results at all?

Because they quantify current assumptions, expose break-even pressure, identify
which uncertainties dominate, and provide a basis for designing the next data
and market experiments.
