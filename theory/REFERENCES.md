# Core references from the working paper

This is a convenience index, not a replacement for the bibliography in the
paper. Bibliographic details below are taken from the working paper.

## McKean-Vlasov control and Wasserstein dynamic programming

- Pham, H. and Wei, X. (2016). Dynamic programming for optimal control of
  stochastic McKean-Vlasov dynamics. arXiv:1604.04057.
- Djete, M. F., Possamai, D. and Tan, X. (2019). McKean-Vlasov optimal control:
  the dynamic programming principle. arXiv:1907.08860.
- Burzoni, M., Ignazio, V., Reppen, A. M. and Soner, H. M. (2019). Viscosity
  solutions for controlled McKean-Vlasov jump-diffusions. arXiv:1909.12337.
- Soner, H. M. and Yan, Q. (2022). Viscosity solutions for McKean-Vlasov
  control on a torus. arXiv:2212.11053.
- Bayraktar, E. et al. (2025). Viscosity solutions of fully second-order HJB
  equations in the Wasserstein space. arXiv:2501.01612.

## Unbalanced transport and Hellinger-Kantorovich geometry

- Chizat, L., Peyre, G., Schmitzer, B. and Vialard, F.-X. (2015). Unbalanced
  optimal transport: dynamic and Kantorovich formulation. arXiv:1508.05216.
- Liero, M., Mielke, A. and Savare, G. (2015). Optimal entropy-transport
  problems and a new Hellinger-Kantorovich distance between positive measures.
  arXiv:1508.07941.
- Chizat, L. et al. (2016). Scaling algorithms for unbalanced transport
  problems. arXiv:1607.05816.
- Liero, M., Mielke, A. and Savare, G. (2022). Fine properties of geodesics and
  geodesic lambda-convexity for the Hellinger-Kantorovich distance.
  arXiv:2208.14299.

## Bayesian learning, experimental design, and robustness

- Meijer, T. J. and Rantzer, A. (2026). Dual control: on
  exploration-exploitation in linear systems. arXiv:2608.20073.
- Li, Z. et al. (2023). Dual control of exploration and exploitation for
  auto-optimisation control with active learning. arXiv:2301.11984.
- Foster, A. et al. (2019). Variational Bayesian optimal experimental design.
  arXiv:1903.05480.
- Rainforth, T. et al. (2024). Modern Bayesian experimental design.
  Statistical Science 39(1):100-114; arXiv:2302.14545.
- Esfahani, P. M. and Kuhn, D. (2015). Data-driven distributionally robust
  optimization using the Wasserstein metric. arXiv:1505.05116.
- Kennedy, M. C. and O'Hagan, A. (2001). Bayesian calibration of computer
  models. JRSS B 63(3):425-464.
